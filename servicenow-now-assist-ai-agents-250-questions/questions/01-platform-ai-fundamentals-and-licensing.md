### 01-001. What is Now Assist?

**Level:** Beginner

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

The name for ServiceNow's generative-AI-driven solutions and workflows that improve productivity and the employee and customer experience on the Now Platform.

</details>

### 01-002. What is ServiceNow Otto?

**Level:** Beginner

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

The new AI experience brand announced in 2026. It brings together Now Assist, Moveworks and the AI Experience platform behind one conversational entry point.

</details>

### 01-003. Which release shows the Otto branding in the Now Assist documentation?

**Level:** Beginner

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

The Australia release. Its release notes say Otto is the new AI experience brand and that product entitlements remain unchanged.

</details>

### 01-004. What is Now LLM?

**Level:** Beginner

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

ServiceNow's own large language model service, which powers most out-of-the-box Now Assist skills.

</details>

### 01-005. Which languages does Now LLM support natively, according to the Now Assist FAQ?

**Level:** Intermediate

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

English, Spanish, Japanese, French, German, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Dutch and Canadian French. Check the current FAQ because support expands over time.

</details>

### 01-006. How does Now Assist handle languages Now LLM does not support natively?

**Level:** Intermediate

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

Through Dynamic Translation. ServiceNow provides an out-of-the-box translation service for Dynamic Translation when it is used only for Now Assist.

</details>

### 01-007. What is a skill in Now Assist?

**Level:** Beginner

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

A packaged generative AI capability, such as incident summarization, with a prompt, a model, inputs, outputs and security settings that performs one task.

</details>

### 01-008. How is Now Assist consumption measured?

**Level:** Beginner

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

In assists. Different skills and uses consume different numbers of assists, as shown on the assist rate card.

</details>

### 01-009. Are assists charged when an LLM request returns an error?

**Level:** Intermediate

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

According to the Now Assist FAQ, no. This includes out-of-the-box Now LLM skills (even when Now Assist Guardian guardrails trigger) and custom skills that use third-party LLMs.

</details>

### 01-015. Can customers use LLMs other than Now LLM?

**Level:** Intermediate

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

Yes. Options include OEM models through model provider flexibility, spokes, the generic LLM connector, and the Generative AI Controller. Separate third-party subscription charges may apply.

</details>

### 01-016. What is model provider flexibility?

**Level:** Intermediate

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

The ability to choose providers other than Now LLM for Now Assist features. From Yokohama Patch 6, models such as Gemini, Azure OpenAI and Claude can be used for many out-of-the-box skills.

</details>

### 01-020. What is the Generative AI Controller?

**Level:** Beginner

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

The connection layer between the Now Platform and large language models, used for out-of-the-box and custom generative AI use cases.

</details>

### 01-021. How do Now Assist and the Generative AI Controller differ?

**Level:** Intermediate

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

Now Assist is the set of products and skills delivered by ServiceNow. The Generative AI Controller is the connector framework that links the platform to LLMs, including external ones, for custom use cases.

</details>

### 01-022. What are the two main ways to use generative AI on an instance?

**Level:** Intermediate

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

Out-of-the-box skills from Now Assist products, and custom use cases built with Now Assist Skill Kit or the Generative AI Controller.

</details>

### 01-023. Does Now Assist address domain separation?

**Level:** Intermediate

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

Yes. The FAQ covers domain separation in the AI Admin Hub, the Generative AI Controller and AI Search.

</details>

### 01-024. Why does it matter that entitlements are unchanged by the Otto rename?

**Level:** Intermediate

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

Customers do not need a new license only because of the new brand. Access to specific features still depends on the entitlements they hold.

</details>

### 01-025. What are Xanadu, Yokohama, Zurich and Australia?

**Level:** Intermediate

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

ServiceNow platform release family names, in that order. Australia is the 2026 release, so check which family your instance runs before relying on a feature.

</details>
