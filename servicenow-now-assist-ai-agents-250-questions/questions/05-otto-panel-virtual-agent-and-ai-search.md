# 05. Otto Panel, Virtual Agent and AI Search

### 05-001. What is the Otto panel?

**Level:** Beginner

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

The conversational panel where users interact with AI and see results from agents. Earlier documentation calls it the Now Assist panel.

</details>

### 05-002. What does the Otto panel require?

**Level:** Beginner

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

The Next Experience UI Framework must be enabled, according to the AI Agent release notes.

</details>

### 05-003. What is Virtual Agent?

**Level:** Beginner

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

ServiceNow's conversational interface for guided topics and self-service.

</details>

### 05-004. What changed for Virtual Agent topic citations in Australia?

**Level:** Intermediate

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

Citations from Now Assist Multi-Content Response Genius Results open the topic in the Now Assist panel so the conversation can continue there.

</details>

### 05-005. What is AI Search?

**Level:** Beginner

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

The ServiceNow search experience that uses AI to return relevant results across your content.

</details>

### 05-006. What is Now Assist in AI Search?

**Level:** Intermediate

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

A capability that provides a Q&A Genius Result card with answers based on content in your system.

</details>

### 05-007. What is a Genius Result?

**Level:** Intermediate

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

A rich result card that gives a direct answer or action instead of only a list of links.

</details>

### 05-008. Why re-index AI Search sources?

**Level:** Intermediate

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

Search results and generated answers depend on the index. Re-indexing picks up new or changed content.

</details>

### 05-009. What is new in the September 2026 Otto panel for workspaces?

**Level:** Intermediate

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

Users can switch between records in panel workspaces without losing the conversation context.

</details>

### 05-010. What does Otto combine?

**Level:** Beginner

<details>

Now Assist, Moveworks and the ServiceNow AI Experience platform in one interface.

</details>

### 05-011. Where was Otto available at launch, according to coverage of Knowledge 2026?

<details>

Through EmployeeWorks and AI Control Tower, with plans to extend across the portfolio during the year.

### 05-012. Why do citations matter in generated answers?

**Level:** Intermediate

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

They show where an answer came from, which builds trust and lets users verify it.

</details>

### 05-013. Can citations be turned off?

**Level:** Intermediate

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

Yes, for specific agents or agentic workflows in AI Agent Studio where citations are not needed.

</details>

### 05-014. When should you build a Virtual Agent topic instead of an agent?

**Level:** Advanced

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

When the steps are known and fixed. Agents suit open-ended, multi-step tasks that need reasoning.

</details>

### 05-015. How do you keep search answers accurate?

**Level:** Intermediate

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

Maintain good knowledge, keep indexes current, and make sure content permissions are correct.

</details>

### 05-016. How do permissions affect what a user sees in search answers?

**Level:** Intermediate

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

Answers should be drawn only from content the user is allowed to access, so access rules must be correct.

</details>

### 05-017. What are display locations for agents?

**Level:** Intermediate

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

The places where an agent is available to users, such as the panel or Virtual Agent, set during Guided Setup.

</details>

### 05-018. Where does output from an agentic workflow appear?

**Level:** Intermediate

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

In the Otto (Now Assist) panel.

</details>

### 05-019. What does a user do if an answer looks wrong?

**Level:** Intermediate

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

Check the citation, give feedback, and escalate or search again. Administrators then review sources and configuration.

</details>

### 05-020. How would you troubleshoot different answers between Virtual Agent and AI Search?

**Level:** Advanced

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

Check plugin versions, re-index the search sources, compare the content each channel can access, and review each channel's configuration.

</details>
