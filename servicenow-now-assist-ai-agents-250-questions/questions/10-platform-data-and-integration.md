### 10-001. What is Workflow Data Fabric?

### 10-002. What is the Context Engine?

**Level:** Beginner

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

A platform capability that gives AI shared operational context, working alongside the CMDB.

</details>

### 10-003. Why does the CMDB matter for AI agents?

**Level:** Beginner

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

It provides the relationships between services and assets, which helps agents reason about impact and dependencies.

</details>

### 10-004. What is retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)?

**Level:** Intermediate

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

A pattern that retrieves relevant content and gives it to the model so answers rest on your data.

</details>

### 10-005. Why do documents get split into chunks for retrieval?

**Level:** Intermediate

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

Smaller pieces can be matched and fetched precisely, which improves relevance and reduces unneeded context.

</details>

### 10-008. What is MCP?

**Level:** Intermediate

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

</details>

### 10-009. How does ServiceNow use MCP?

**Level:** Intermediate

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

AI Agent Fabric uses MCP to connect agents with external tools and resources, and an MCP server exposes ServiceNow capabilities to outside agents.

</details>

### 10-011. What is Data Catalog in the Australia release?

<details>

</details>

### 10-012. How does data quality limit AI results?

**Level:** Advanced

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

Incomplete or stale data leads to wrong summaries, answers and actions, so data stewardship comes first.

</details>

### 10-013. What is the idea of zero-copy data access?

**Level:** Advanced

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

Working with data where it lives instead of copying it, which reduces duplication and keeps it fresher.

</details>

### 10-014. What is Knowledge Center?
