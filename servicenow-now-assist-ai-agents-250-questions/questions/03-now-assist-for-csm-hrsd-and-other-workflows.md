**Level:** Intermediate

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

</details>

### 03-015. What is the difference between agent-facing and requester-facing AI?

**Level:** Intermediate

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

Agent-facing AI assists fulfillers who review the output. Requester-facing AI answers users directly, often without human review.

</details>

### 03-016. Which is riskier, agent-facing or requester-facing AI, and why?

**Level:** Advanced

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

Requester-facing, because no human checks the answer before it is used. Apply stricter guardrails, citations, feedback and monitoring.

</details>
