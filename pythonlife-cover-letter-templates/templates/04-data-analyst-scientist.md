# Data Analyst and Data Scientist

**Use when:** You are applying for data roles and want to show the path from question to business decision.
**Length:** About 230 words

## Letter

```text
{{YOUR_NAME}}
{{EMAIL}} · {{PHONE}} · github.com/{{GITHUB}}

{{DATE}}

Dear {{HIRING_MANAGER}},

I am applying for the {{ROLE}} role at {{COMPANY}}. Your work on {{SPECIFIC_THING}} depends on turning data into decisions, which is the part of analysis I enjoy most.

In my most recent project, {{PROJECT_1}}, the question was {{BUSINESS_QUESTION}}. I used Python ({{TECH_USED}}) to {{WHAT_YOU_DID}}. The analysis showed {{FINDING}}, and the recommendation led to {{DECISION_OR_RESULT}}.

I care about the full pipeline, not just the model: cleaning messy data, checking assumptions, and presenting findings so that non-technical colleagues can act on them. I have {{COMMUNICATION_PROOF}}.

Your posting mentions {{REQUIREMENT_1}} and {{REQUIREMENT_2}}. My notebooks are public at github.com/{{GITHUB}}, with the questions, methods and limitations written out.

I would be glad to discuss how I could support {{TEAM_OR_PRODUCT}}.

Sincerely,
{{YOUR_NAME}}
```

## Tailoring notes

- Structure the proof as question, method, finding, decision. Hiring managers read for business impact.
- Naming limitations in your public notebooks shows maturity.
- Mention a time you explained results to a non-technical audience.
