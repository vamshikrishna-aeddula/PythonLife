# Referral and Cold Outreach

**Use when:** There is no open role, or a contact suggested you reach out. Send by email or LinkedIn.
**Length:** About 120 words

## Letter

```text
{{YOUR_NAME}}
{{EMAIL}} · {{PHONE}} · github.com/{{GITHUB}}

{{DATE}}

Dear {{HIRING_MANAGER}},

{{REFERRER_OR_REASON_FOR_WRITING}}, so I wanted to reach out directly.

I am a Python developer focused on {{FOCUS_AREA}}. Recently I built {{PROJECT_1}}, which {{RESULT_OR_USERS}}. I admire {{COMPANY}}'s work on {{SPECIFIC_THING}} and would like to contribute as a {{ROLE}}.

If a role opens or you are hiring, I would value a short conversation of 15 minutes at your convenience. My work is at github.com/{{GITHUB}}, and my CV is attached.

Thank you for your time.

Sincerely,
{{YOUR_NAME}}
```

## Tailoring notes

- Keep it under 150 words. The goal is a reply, not a hire.
- Open with the connection: a mutual contact, a talk, an article, an open-source contribution.
- Ask for 15 minutes and offer easy options. Do not ask for a job outright.
- Subject line idea: `{{REFERRER}} suggested I get in touch about Python roles`.
