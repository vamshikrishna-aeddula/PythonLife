# Student and Internship

**Use when:** You are a student applying for an internship or placement with limited work history.
**Length:** About 200 words

## Letter

```text
{{YOUR_NAME}}
{{EMAIL}} · {{PHONE}} · github.com/{{GITHUB}}

{{DATE}}

Dear {{HIRING_MANAGER}},

I am a {{YEAR_OF_STUDY}} student studying {{DEGREE}} at {{UNIVERSITY}}, and I am applying for the {{ROLE}} internship at {{COMPANY}}. I am available from {{START_DATE}} to {{END_DATE}}.

In my coursework I have worked with {{TECH_USED}}, and outside class I built {{PROJECT_1}}, a {{WHAT_IT_DOES}}. {{RESULT_OR_LESSON}}. I also {{ACTIVITY}}, which helped me practice {{SKILL}}.

I am drawn to {{COMPANY}} because {{WHY_THIS_COMPANY}}. During the internship I hope to learn {{LEARNING_GOAL}} and contribute to {{TEAM_OR_PRODUCT}} by {{CONTRIBUTION}}.

My code is available at github.com/{{GITHUB}}, and my references can speak to my reliability and curiosity. Thank you for considering my application.

Sincerely,
{{YOUR_NAME}}
```

## Tailoring notes

- State availability dates in the first paragraph. It answers a key screening question immediately.
- Group projects count. Say what you personally built.
- A learning goal plus a contribution shows you think about what you give, not only what you get.
