# Junior Python Developer

**Use when:** You are applying for your first Python role and your proof is mostly personal or portfolio projects.
**Length:** About 220 words

## Letter

```text
{{YOUR_NAME}}
{{EMAIL}} · {{PHONE}} · github.com/{{GITHUB}}

{{DATE}}

Dear {{HIRING_MANAGER}},

I noticed that {{COMPANY}} recently {{SPECIFIC_THING}}. That is the kind of problem I want to work on, which is why I am applying for the {{ROLE}} role.

Over the past {{MONTHS}} months I built {{PROJECT_1}}, a {{WHAT_IT_DOES}}. It is tested with pytest, documented, and {{RESULT_OR_USERS}}. I also {{SECOND_PROOF}}, which taught me {{LESSON}}.

Your posting asks for {{REQUIREMENT_1}} and {{REQUIREMENT_2}}. I used {{TECH_USED}} for both in {{PROJECT_1}}, and the code is public at github.com/{{GITHUB}} so you can judge it directly.

I would welcome a conversation about how I can contribute to {{TEAM_OR_PRODUCT}}. Thank you for your time.

Sincerely,
{{YOUR_NAME}}
```

## Tailoring notes

- Replace `{{SPECIFIC_THING}}` with something real from the company blog, release notes or job post. This one sentence separates your letter from a mass application.
- Pick the project that best matches the posting, not your biggest one.
- Put a number in `{{RESULT_OR_USERS}}` where you can: users, time saved, test coverage, stars.
