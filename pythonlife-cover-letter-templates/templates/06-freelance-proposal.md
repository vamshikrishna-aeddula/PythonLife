# Freelance Proposal

**Use when:** You are pitching a client for a project, either in reply to a brief or after a first conversation.
**Length:** About 230 words

## Letter

```text
{{YOUR_NAME}}
{{EMAIL}} · {{PHONE}} · github.com/{{GITHUB}}

{{DATE}}

Dear {{HIRING_MANAGER}},

Thank you for sharing the details of {{PROJECT_NAME}}. In short, you need {{CLIENT_PROBLEM}}, and the goal is {{CLIENT_GOAL}}.

Here is how I would approach it:

1. {{STEP_1}}
2. {{STEP_2}}
3. {{STEP_3}}

You would receive {{DELIVERABLES}}, delivered in {{TIMELINE}}, for {{PRICE_OR_RANGE}}.

I have solved a similar problem before: for {{PAST_CLIENT_OR_PROJECT}} I {{PAST_RESULT}}. You can see related work at github.com/{{GITHUB}}.

To confirm the scope, I have two questions: {{QUESTION_1}} and {{QUESTION_2}}. If the plan looks right, I can start on {{START_DATE}}.

Sincerely,
{{YOUR_NAME}}
```

## Tailoring notes

- Restate the client problem in your own words. It proves you listened.
- Always include timeline and price or range. Vague proposals lose to clear ones.
- End with specific questions. They move the conversation forward and show you are already thinking about the work.
- This template skips the formal greeting block on purpose: edit the header to suit the channel.
