# Career Switcher

**Use when:** You are moving into Python from another field and need to connect past experience to the new role.
**Length:** About 230 words

## Letter

```text
{{YOUR_NAME}}
{{EMAIL}} · {{PHONE}} · github.com/{{GITHUB}}

{{DATE}}

Dear {{HIRING_MANAGER}},

After {{YEARS}} years in {{PREVIOUS_FIELD}}, I moved into Python development because {{REASON_FOR_SWITCH}}. I am applying for the {{ROLE}} role at {{COMPANY}} because {{WHY_THIS_COMPANY}}.

My previous work gave me skills that carry over directly. At {{PREVIOUS_EMPLOYER}} I {{TRANSFERABLE_ACHIEVEMENT}}, which means I already know how to {{TRANSFERABLE_SKILL}} under real deadlines.

To build the technical side, I completed {{COURSE_OR_BOOTCAMP}} and built {{PROJECT_1}}, a {{WHAT_IT_DOES}} that {{RESULT_OR_USERS}}. The code is at github.com/{{GITHUB}}.

I would bring both a working command of {{TECH_USED}} and a clear understanding of {{DOMAIN_KNOWLEDGE}}, which is useful for {{TEAM_OR_PRODUCT}}. I would be glad to talk about where I can help most.

Sincerely,
{{YOUR_NAME}}
```

## Tailoring notes

- Lead with the reason for the switch in one honest sentence. Avoid apologizing for your background.
- Domain knowledge is your edge. A former nurse building health software, or an accountant building finance tools, is a strong story.
- Keep the career change to one paragraph. Spend the rest on what you can do now.
